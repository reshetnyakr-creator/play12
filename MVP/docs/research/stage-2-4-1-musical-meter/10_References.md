# Источники

- [W3C MusicXML 4.0, описание версии](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/) и [Notation Basics](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/tutorial/notation-basics/). Элементы [attributes](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/attributes/), [divisions](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/divisions/), [time](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/time/), [beats](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/beats/), [beat-type](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/beat-type/), [interchangeable](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/interchangeable/), [senza-misura](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/senza-misura/), [measure](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/measure-partwise/).
- W3C, элементы [direction](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/direction/), [direction-type](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/direction-type/), [metronome](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/metronome/), [beat-unit](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/beat-unit/), [beat-unit-dot](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/beat-unit-dot/), [per-minute](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/per-minute/), [sound](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/sound/) и [offset](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/offset/).
- W3C, элементы [duration](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/duration/), [type](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/type/), [dot](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/dot/), [time-modification](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/time-modification/), [tuplet](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/tuplet/), [beam](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/beam/), [backup](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/backup/), [forward](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/elements/forward/) и [пример time-modification](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/musicxml-reference/examples/time-modification-element/).
- Gotham et al., Open Music Theory, [Meter](https://open-musictheory.github.io/docs/fundamentals/meter/), разделы о группировке и подразделении. University of Iowa, [Meter](https://pressbooks.uiowa.edu/twentieth-and-twenty-first-century-music/chapter/meter/), раздел Asymmetrical Meter и пример Bartók №115 для вариантов 5/8.
- Large & Jones, 1999, [The Dynamics of Attending](https://musicdynamicslab.media.uconn.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/433/2016/03/LargeJones1999Ahedits.pdf), pp. 119–159. Large & Palmer, [Dynamic models for musical rhythm perception and coordination](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10229831/), раздел Dynamic attending and perception-action coordination.
- Hoffman, Pelto, White, 1996, [Takadimi: A Beat-Oriented System of Rhythm Pedagogy](https://musescore.org/sites/musescore.org/files/2024-10/Takadimi%20Article.pdf), разделы Beat Orientation, Simple and Compound Division. Педагогический прецедент, эксперимент Play12 требуется отдельно.
- MuseScore Studio Handbook, [Time signatures](https://handbook.musescore.org/notation/rhythm-meter-and-measures/time-signatures), разделы о свойствах размера и Beam Groups. [Beams](https://handbook.musescore.org/en_gb/notation/rhythm-meter-and-measures/beams) описывает редактируемое ребрение поверх умолчаний.
- Для дополнительного покрытия из доступных материалов использовать примеры Bartók №115 из учебника и составного размера из W3C как цели ручной верификации. В этом пакете не подтверждены скачивание и лицензия дополнительных сторонних партитур в MusicXML. Синтетические fixtures покрывают заданные комбинации без распространения аранжировок из рабочего корпуса.
